Privacy Policy

How FarmsCity handles contact details, platform interest, and operational information.

This policy explains what information we collect from visitors, demo requests, and prospective customers, and how we use it to support FarmsCity services.

Information we collect

We may collect names, email addresses, organization details, inquiry content, and basic website interaction data when you contact FarmsCity, request a demo, or subscribe to updates.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to inquiries, schedule product conversations, improve the website, share relevant platform updates, and support business development for FarmsCity.

Data sharing

We do not sell your information. We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate the website, manage communications, or support product delivery, subject to appropriate controls.

Data retention

We retain contact and inquiry records for as long as needed to manage relationships, evaluate partnerships, comply with legal obligations, and maintain business records.

Your choices

You can request updates, corrections, or removal of your contact information from our outreach lists by emailing us. You can also opt out of newsletter communications at any time.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact contact@farmscity.org.
